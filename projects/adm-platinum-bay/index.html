Kamala fills the bay directly south of Surin, separated from it by a coastal headland that the locals call Laem Singh. The area has shifted character noticeably over the past five years. What used to be a quiet Muslim fishing village is now home to one of Asia top ranked beach clubs at Cafe del Mar, the Phuket International Academy school, and a steady stream of branded condo and villa launches in the hills behind the bay. The foreign community here is genuinely mixed: long term European retirees who arrived in the 1990s still live in older Kamala houses, while Russian, Chinese and Israeli families have moved into the newer hillside developments. The result is a place that feels less polished than Bang Tao but more residential than Patong. Daily life centres on the main road parallel to the beach, with a fresh market, small Thai restaurants, two minimarts, dive shops and an outsized number of property agents. The beach itself is a long, gentle arc of yellow sand that stays calm even in the green season inside the bay. Schools are well covered with PIA on the doorstep and Kajonkiet a short drive over the hill to Patong, while the Cherngtalay cluster sits ten minutes north. Healthcare follows the same pattern as Surin, with Bangkok Hospital Phuket the main private facility. Kamala suits buyers who want a real neighbourhood feel with strong beach club and dining options.

Kamala Beach Road with cafes and dive shops; Cafe del Mar Phuket area (beachfront events and dining); Plaza del Mar boutique mall; Kamala Fresh Market on the main road; Cherngtalay corridor reachable in 10 minutes north.

Kamala Hospital (public, 1 km); Bangkok Hospital Phuket (20 km, JCI-accredited); Bangkok Hospital Siriroj in Phuket Town (22 km).

Cafe del Mar Phuket (one of the top ranked beach clubs in the world), HQ Beach Lounge at Hyatt Regency, Rockfish Restaurant at Cape Sienna (panoramic sunset views), Shimmer at Twinpalms Phuket Kamala, Vala Beach Club at InterContinental Phuket Resort.
