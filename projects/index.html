New projects in Thailand — FAQ

What is an off-plan project?

A residence that is under construction or about to start. Developers sell units at earlier-stage prices with a payment plan tied to construction.

What do I get from you?

Layouts, price lists, payment plans and completion dates for each project, plus viewings of the show units and the site.

Can a project unit be rented out?

Many projects run a rental programme or allow private rental. We share the expected occupancy and terms per project.
