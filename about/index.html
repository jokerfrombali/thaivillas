What is Thai Villas?+

Thai Villas is a villa rental service in Thailand: a local team plus an AI search that matches you to handpicked villas in Phuket, Koh Samui, Pattaya, Bangkok, Krabi and Chiang Mai — for daily, monthly and yearly stays, with concierge help before and during your trip.

How do I book a villa?+

Describe your stay in plain words in our AI search, or message us on WhatsApp at +66 00 000 0000. We confirm availability and the exact price with the villa, send you a written confirmation, and you secure your dates with a deposit. Prices are on request while we're getting started.

How do deposits and payments work?+

Short stays are usually secured with a booking deposit, with the balance due before check-in. Monthly and yearly stays typically include a refundable security deposit, returned after check-out once the villa is checked. Every amount, plus the cancellation terms, is written in your confirmation before you pay.

Can I rent for a month or a year?+

Yes — and many of our guests do. Long stays usually come at a lower effective nightly rate. We help with what matters over a longer stay: how electricity and water are billed, cleaning schedules, Wi-Fi good enough for video calls, and schools, gyms or co-working nearby.

Do I need a visa for a long stay?+

It depends on your passport and how long you stay. Many nationalities can enter visa-free for a limited period, and there are longer options such as tourist, education, retirement and long-term visas. Rules change, so please check with the Thai embassy or official sources — we're happy to share what other long-stay guests have done, but we don't provide visa services.

When is the best time to visit?+

High season runs roughly November to April: dry, sunny and busiest — book early, especially around Christmas, New Year and Songkran in April. Koh Samui on the Gulf side is often driest from January to August, while Phuket and Krabi on the Andaman coast see more rain from May to October. Green season means lower rates and quieter beaches.
