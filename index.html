Who we are

Phuket first.
The simple way to rent a villa.

Thai Villas is a villa rental service for Phuket and the rest of Thailand. We focus on the island's west-coast beaches — from Mai Khao and Bang Tao in the north down to Kata, Nai Harn and Rawai in the south — plus Koh Samui, Pattaya, Bangkok, Krabi, Koh Phangan, Hua Hin and Chiang Mai.

You can rent by the night for a holiday or by the month for a longer season. Either way you deal with real people on WhatsApp, get the price for your dates in writing and pay no booking-site fee on top.

Planning something bigger? We also arrange yacht charters, helicopter transfers, supercars and superbikes, so the whole trip sits in one conversation.
