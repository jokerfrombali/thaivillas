For sale in Phuket — FAQ

Can foreigners buy property in Thailand?

Foreigners can own a condominium unit outright (freehold) within the foreign quota of a building. Villas and land are usually bought on a long-term lease or through a structure your lawyer sets up. Each listing states the ownership type when known.

How do viewings work?

Message us on WhatsApp with the listings you like. We arrange viewings in person or by video call and send the documents for each property.

Are the prices final?

Prices are the asking or developer prices at the time of listing in Thai baht. Send us a message to confirm the current price and availability.
