About the property

Kora Beach Resort Phuket | Two Bedroom Executive Suite | 2 bedroom

Kora Phuket is a modern boutique hotel located in the Bangtao area, just a few minutes’ walk from the beach. It’s perfect for a peaceful and comfortable stay close to the best restaurants, cafés, and beach clubs. The hotel features a sleek contemporary design and offers cozy rooms with balconies, some with direct pool access. On-site amenities include a spacious swimming pool, restaurant, bar, and fitness center.

Thanks to its convenient location, guests can easily reach Bangtao Beach, the popular Catch and Carpe Diem beach clubs, as well as the main street with its cafés, shops, and supermarkets.

Comfortable apartments featuring two bedrooms, a separate living area, and a fully equipped kitchen — an excellent choice for long stays or family getaways.
