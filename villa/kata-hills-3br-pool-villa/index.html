About this villa

Perched on a green hillside above Kata, this three-bedroom villa is built around a private pool with views over the Andaman Sea. The open-plan living and dining area folds out onto a shaded terrace, so breakfast, sunset drinks and lazy afternoons all happen by the water.

Each bedroom has its own bathroom and air conditioning, and there is a quiet workspace with reliable Wi-Fi for anyone who needs to stay connected. The fully equipped kitchen makes longer stays easy, and parking on site means beach days at Kata and Kata Noi are only a short drive away.
