Renting a pool villa in Thailand

This catalogue brings together private villas for rent across Thailand, starting with Phuket — the island with the widest choice of pool villas, from family houses in Bang Tao and Layan to sea-view homes above Kata and long-stay favourites in Nai Harn and Rawai. Koh Samui, Pattaya and other destinations are being added.

Use the filters to narrow the list by beach area, bedrooms, guests, amenities and rental period. Every villa can be booked for a holiday by the night, and many also by the month; send the dates and we come back on WhatsApp with availability and the price.

Daily or monthly

Two ways to rent: daily stays for holidays, and monthly stays for anyone spending a season in Thailand. Monthly rates are set per villa and cover a fixed term with the conditions spelled out in the quote.

When to come

On Phuket, Krabi and the Andaman coast the dry, calm season runs from November to April. Koh Samui and Koh Phangan are usually driest from about January to August, and Chiang Mai is most pleasant from November to February.

How booking works

Pick a villa, choose dates and send the request — it lands on our WhatsApp. We confirm availability, the total and the conditions in writing before anything is paid. Nothing is charged on this website.

Renting a villa in Thailand — FAQ

How do I book a villa in Thailand?

Open a villa page, choose your dates and guests and send the request. It comes straight to our WhatsApp and we reply with availability and the price for those dates.

Which area of Phuket is best for a villa?

Bang Tao and Layan suit families and groups who want space and amenities; Kata and Karon are sociable beach towns; Nai Harn and Rawai are favourites for long stays; Mai Khao is quiet and close to the airport. Each beach has its own page with the villas there.

Can I rent a villa by the month?

Yes. Choose "Monthly" in the filters to see villas offered for monthly stays, or see the monthly rentals guide. Monthly rates and what they include are set per villa.

Why is the price "On request"?

The villas shown are sample listings while we onboard properties, and rates depend on dates and length of stay. Send your dates and we quote the exact price.

Do you offer anything besides villas?

Yes — yacht charters, helicopter flights, supercars, superbikes and concierge services in Phuket. See the VIP section.
