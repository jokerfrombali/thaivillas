Phuket villa rentals

Renting a villa in Phuket

Phuket is Thailand's largest island and the country's main villa destination. Almost all of the beaches face west onto the Andaman Sea, so sunsets come with the view, and the choice runs from compact pool townhouses to hillside estates with staff.

The north-west — Mai Khao, Nai Yang, Layan, Bang Tao and Surin — is quieter and close to the airport, with Bang Tao's Cherng Talay as the island's villa capital. The middle of the coast is the busiest: Kamala, Patong, Karon and Kata. The south — Kata Noi, Nai Harn, Rawai and Chalong — is greener and calmer, and the favourite of long-stay guests.

High season runs from November to April, with calm seas and dry weather; the green season from May to October brings showers and swell on the west coast, but also more choice and a quieter island. Villas can be rented daily for a holiday or monthly for a season.

West coast beaches

Bang Tao, Surin and Kamala combine beaches with full neighbourhoods; Patong is the nightlife centre; Karon and Kata are sociable, walkable beach towns, with surf at Kata from May to October.

North and south

Mai Khao, Nai Yang and Layan in the north are quiet and 5–25 minutes from the airport. Nai Harn, Rawai and Chalong in the south are about an hour away, with island boats, markets and a big long-stay community.

Seasons

The calmest sea and driest weather on Phuket's west coast run from November to April. From May to October the green season brings showers, bigger swell and red-flag days on some beaches, but also greener hills, fewer people and better availability — a good time for monthly stays.

Daily or monthly

Daily stays suit holidays of a few days to a few weeks. Monthly stays are priced per villa for a month or more — popular in Bang Tao, Nai Harn, Rawai and Chalong, especially in the green season.

Getting around

Phuket International Airport is in the north of the island. A car or scooter is the practical way to get around; taxis and ride-hailing apps work across the island, and we can arrange transfers.

Phuket villa rental — FAQ

How do I book a villa in Phuket?

Open a villa, choose your dates and send the request — it comes straight to us on WhatsApp. We reply with availability and the price for your dates, and confirm everything in writing before you pay.

Can I rent a villa in Phuket by the month?

Yes. Every villa is offered for daily stays, and many also for monthly or longer stays. Monthly rates and what they include (electricity, cleaning, pool care) are set per villa — filter by "Monthly" or ask us and we will list the options.

Which Phuket beach is best for a villa?

For families and amenities, Bang Tao and Layan; for a lively beach town, Kata and Karon; for nightlife, Patong; for long stays, Nai Harn, Rawai and Chalong; for peace and quick airport access, Mai Khao and Nai Yang.

When is the best time to stay in Phuket?

November to April is high season, with calm seas and dry weather on the west coast. May to October is the green season: showers, surf at Kata and Karon, and quieter beaches — good for monthly stays.

How do I get to Phuket?

Phuket International Airport is in the north of the island, with domestic and international flights. Mai Khao and Layan are 5–25 minutes away, Bang Tao about 30, Patong about 45 and the southern beaches about an hour by car.

Why do the villas show "On request" instead of a price?
