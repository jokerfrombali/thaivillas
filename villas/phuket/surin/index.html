Villas in Surin, Phuket

Surin is a short, steep beach tucked between Bang Tao and Kamala, framed by headlands and known for clear water in the dry season. The hills around it hold some of the island's most upscale villas and boutique hotels, including the cliff-top houses on the road towards Kamala.

The beach itself is compact, with a park and a few casual restaurants behind it. For shopping and everyday errands, Cherng Talay and Bang Tao are five to ten minutes away.

Who is Surin for?

Couples and families who want a beautiful small beach and a quiet, polished setting close to Bang Tao's amenities.

Getting there

Roughly 30 minutes from the airport, about 10 minutes to Bang Tao or Kamala and around 20 minutes to Patong.

When to go

The calmest sea and driest weather on Phuket's west coast run from November to April. From May to October the green season brings showers, bigger swell and red-flag days on some beaches, but also greener hills, fewer people and better availability — a good time for monthly stays.
