Bangkok rentals

Villas in Bangkok, Thailand

Bangkok is Thailand's capital and largest city. For house-style living, guests usually look at neighbourhoods such as Sukhumvit, Thonglor–Ekkamai and Sathorn, close to the BTS Skytrain and MRT lines, with international schools, hospitals and restaurants nearby.

Bangkok is above all a monthly destination: business trips, relocation, school terms or a comfortable base while exploring the country. The most pleasant weather is the cool season from roughly November to February; April and May are the hottest months.

Who is Bangkok for?

Professionals, relocating families and long-stay guests who need a home in the city rather than a hotel room.

Getting there

Suvarnabhumi and Don Mueang airports both connect to the centre; journey times range from about 30 to 60 minutes depending on traffic.

When to go

Cool season November–February, hot season March–May, rainy season roughly June–October.

Bangkok villa rental — FAQ

How do I book a villa in Bangkok?

Open a villa, choose your dates and send the request — it comes straight to us on WhatsApp. We reply with availability and the price for your dates, and confirm everything in writing before you pay.

Yes. Every villa is offered for daily stays, and many also for monthly or longer stays. Monthly rates and what they include (electricity, cleaning, pool care) are set per villa — filter by "Monthly" or ask us and we will list the options.
