Where to stay in Thailand

Phuket first, then the rest of Thailand

Phuket has the largest choice of private pool villas in the country, spread along a west coast of very different beaches: quiet Mai Khao and Layan in the north, the villa neighbourhoods of Bang Tao and Surin, lively Patong, family-friendly Karon and Kata, and the long-stay south around Nai Harn, Rawai and Chalong.

Beyond Phuket, Koh Samui and Koh Phangan in the Gulf of Thailand have their dry season when the Andaman coast is green; Krabi shares Phuket's rhythm; Pattaya and Hua Hin are easy drives from Bangkok; and Bangkok and Chiang Mai are city bases for monthly living.

Choosing a destination — FAQ

Which destination is best for a villa holiday in Thailand?

Phuket offers the widest choice of pool villas and beaches. Koh Samui is a good alternative, especially from January to August when its weather is at its best.

Where do people stay for a month or longer?

On Phuket, Nai Harn, Rawai, Chalong and Bang Tao are the most popular long-stay areas. Koh Phangan, Pattaya, Bangkok and Chiang Mai are also common monthly bases.
